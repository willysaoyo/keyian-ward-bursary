About Keyian Ward Bursary

Promoting access to education through bursary support.

Our Purpose

The Keyian Ward Bursary Portal is designed to make bursary application services easier to access, more organised and convenient for students and administrators.

Students can apply online, submit supporting documents and follow their application progress.

Application Guidelines

  • Provide accurate personal information.
  • Select your official sub-location.
  • Enter your school or institution details.
  • Upload the required supporting document.
  • Keep your application reference number safe.
Important Notice: Application opening dates, closing dates, eligibility requirements and award decisions should be confirmed through the authorised bursary administration.
